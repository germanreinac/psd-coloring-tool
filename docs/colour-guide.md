# Megalith colour guide

What the flatting tool should put where. Written from the finished Chapter 1 pages (27 pages), the
character sheets and the Chapter 2 line art in the Megalith Drive folder. Colour names are the ones
in `palette.json`. Correct anything here that is wrong: the page colour plans are written against it.

## Who is who
- **Skit** is the grey cat with the two round horns. **Dji** is the bird girl with the coral hair rolls.
  (Earlier versions of the tool had these two names the wrong way round. The colours were right.)
- **Chalky** (Mr Seaworth) is Skit's dad: a big grey cat with round eye patches and glasses.
- Others with a sheet: Skipjack (lilac, eggplant-shaped), Donnie Voyagio (dolphin head, puffy lilac
  jacket), Magus (plum skin, aqua cone hat), Barkus the Deep Dive Bar boss (brown capybara, pink ears).

Eye whites are always a near-white (`Dji shirt` or `pale sky`), never left empty.
Tattoo and pattern lines inside a body part are detail: the whole part takes the body colour.

## The world
- **Great Megalith (the city), daytime**: buildings are pinks and purples. Big walls `candy pink`,
  `mauve`, `lavender`, `hot pink`; roofs and darker faces `dusty mauve`, `plum`; pipes `steel blue`;
  signs `cream` or `custard`; ground and plaza tiles `blush`, `sand`, `rose beige`.
- **Sky**: warm, never blue in the city. `cream` at midday, `blush` or `pale sky` in softer scenes.
  Clouds `pale sky`.
- **The flat (Skit and Dji's home)**: walls `lilac grey`/`mauve`, sofa `candy pink`, floor `dusty mauve`.
- **Sea and water**: `aqua`, `mint`, `jade`; foam `pale sky`. Wooden piers `rose wood` and `dark wood`.
- **Port and boats**: hulls `raspberry`/`coral`/`cream`, sand `sand`, sky `blush`.
- **Night and rain**: the whole panel shifts to blue (`deep blue`, `steel blue`, `teal grey`,
  `charcoal`), with `mint`/`aqua` for lit windows.
- **Dream and slime sequences**: `turquoise`, `jade`, `aqua`, `sky cyan` with `deep blue` shadows.
- **Plain gag panels** (phone-call grids, reaction shots): one flat background per panel, alternating
  `lilac grey` and `mint`.
- **Crowd and background characters**: one skin tone and one clothing colour each, taken from the
  accent colours (`peach`, `coral`, `jade`, `periwinkle`, `terracotta`), so they stay separate.

## How the finished pages are built on top of the flats
- Line art is recoloured (pink-magenta by day, dark blue at night). Flats therefore go under the
  lines and reach to their middle.
- Shading, gradients and light are painted over the flats later. A flat is the local colour of the
  thing in neutral light, not the colour it ends up with in the finished panel.
- Outside the panels stays empty. Elements that break out of a panel border (heads, hands, sound
  effects) still get their colour.
